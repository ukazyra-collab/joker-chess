=== Joker_Chess_K.mp4 — Why chess got boring ===
2018 World Championship: 12 classical games, 12 draws. The strongest engines draw so often that their tournaments force them into strange openings.

Top players prepare their openings at home with engines, so the first moves are often just memory. Chess960, team chess and duck chess all tried to fix it. Joker Chess keeps chess as it is and adds one move: after two moves each there are over 114 million possible games instead of about 197 thousand.

=== Joker_Chess_K2.mp4 — Nobody could win this match ===
1984: the World Championship match went on for five months and 48 games, from 10 September to 8 February. Karpov won five, Kasparov three, 40 were draws, and the match was stopped with no winner.

Top-level chess still ends in a draw most of the time: the video flies over the 2024 and 2022 Candidates. Joker Chess adds one move per game: once, you can put a queen, rook, bishop or knight on any empty square. 197 thousand possible games after two moves each become over 114 million.

=== Joker_Chess_K3.mp4 — Grandmasters don't get mated ===
Grandmasters almost never get checkmated. They see it coming, shake hands and resign, or just agree to a draw. In 2005 a super-GM event in Sofia banned draw offers altogether.

In Joker Chess a lost position isn't always lost: once per game a rook can jump in and block the mate.

=== Joker_Chess_K6.mp4 — The wall that stopped Kasparov ===
2000: Kramnik took the world title from Kasparov with 2 wins, 13 draws and 0 losses, thanks to the Berlin Defence, the "Berlin Wall". In its main line the queens come off by move 8.

That's what opening prep does to chess. Joker Chess adds one move per game, and you can't build a wall against 114 million possible games.

=== Joker_Chess_K7.mp4 — More games than atoms ===
By Claude Shannon's estimate there are more possible chess games than atoms in the observable universe. So why do the best players keep drawing?

Most of those games are full of terrible moves, and in the opening engines have already shown which moves are best: the classical openings are played from memory. Joker Chess adds one move per game: over 57 thousand possible games after one move each, over 114 million after two.

=== Joker_Chess_C.mp4 — Everything one extra move can do ===
What if one piece could teleport, once per game?

That's Joker Chess: normal chess plus one special move for each player. It turns 20 first moves into 240. A rook lands on the queen's file and pins it. A knight drops into a fork. A bishop pins the queen on the diagonal. A rook blocks a check that would be mate. A knight drops next to the king and the mate can't be stopped. And your opponent has a Joker too.
